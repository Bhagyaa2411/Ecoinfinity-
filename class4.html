🌡️ Class 4: Climate Change

The Warming Planet

**Climate change** is when our planet’s weather patterns shift over time. Right now, Earth is getting warmer because humans burn fuels that release gases into the air.

This extra heat is melting ice caps, raising sea levels, and making weather unpredictable. But don’t worry — small actions like saving electricity, planting trees, and using clean energy can make a huge difference.
