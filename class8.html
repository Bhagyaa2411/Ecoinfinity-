📜 Class 8: Environmental Policies

Rules to Protect Nature

**Environmental policies** are rules made by governments and organizations to protect our planet. They help control pollution, protect forests and animals, and encourage people to use renewable energy.

Famous examples include the **Paris Agreement** on climate change and laws that protect endangered species. These policies remind us that caring for nature is not just a choice — it’s a responsibility.
