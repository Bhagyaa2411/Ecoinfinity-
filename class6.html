🌱 Class 6: Sustainable Agriculture

Farming with Care

**Sustainable agriculture** means growing food in a way that’s healthy for both people and the planet. Farmers use fewer chemicals, save water, and protect the soil so that future generations can keep farming.

Crop rotation, organic fertilizers, and natural pest control are some of the techniques that help keep farms productive without harming the environment.
