🌍 Class 9: Future of Our Planet

A Greener Tomorrow

The **future of our planet** depends on the choices we make today. If we take care of our environment, future generations will enjoy clean air, fresh water, and healthy ecosystems.

New technologies like **renewable energy, electric transport, and eco-friendly farming** can help us build a brighter future. Remember: every small action today shapes the Earth of tomorrow!
